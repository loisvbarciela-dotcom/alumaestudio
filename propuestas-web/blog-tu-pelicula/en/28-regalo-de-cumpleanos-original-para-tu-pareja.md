# A unique birthday gift for your partner: ideas that won't fit in a box

A birthday is the only day of the year that belongs to just one person. That is why the gift matters: it has to speak of them, not of the two of you as a couple, nor of what is fashionable this year. If you are looking for a **unique birthday gift for your partner**, these ideas start from a simple question: what would make them feel that you truly know them?

## Ideas to suit your partner

### For someone who plans everything
**A day with nothing to plan.** You take care of everything: breakfast, activity, lunch, a surprise in the afternoon, dinner. Their only job is to let themselves be carried along.

### For someone who collects memories
**The box of the year:** tickets, photos, notes and objects from the past twelve months, arranged like a small archive.

### For someone who dreams of travelling
**A trip with a date** to that place they are always talking about. If it cannot be now, an envelope with the destination and the date already booked.

### For someone with a passion
**An experience with someone they admire** in their field: a lesson with a chef, a guided visit, a workshop.

### For someone who already has everything
**A film in which the two of you are the stars.** From one photograph of each of you, we create a three- or four-minute film that tells your story in another era or another world. With script, direction and editing to cinema standards.

## Milestone birthdays: 30, 40, 50

Birthdays ending in zero deserve something special:

- **30:** a gift that looks forward: a big plan, a trip, a film that imagines your future in outer space.
- **40:** a gift that celebrates who they are today: a party with the important people from each stage of their life.
- **50:** a gift that goes back over their story: a book, a video with messages from lifelong friends, a film in which your story crosses several eras.

## Why a film works for a birthday

- **It puts your partner at the centre** of a cinematic story.
- **It creates a moment:** watching it together, or with friends at the party, is part of the gift.
- **It cannot be compared with anything:** there is no other like it.
- **It adapts to the tone:** epic, tender, funny. For a birthday, humour works very well.

### The tone that works best for a birthday
- **Adventure:** your partner as the protagonist of an expedition, a space mission, a chase in the twenties.
- **Tribute:** scenes that celebrate their passions, their quirks, their favourite phrases.
- **Romantic comedy:** two people searching for each other in another era who keep crossing paths.
