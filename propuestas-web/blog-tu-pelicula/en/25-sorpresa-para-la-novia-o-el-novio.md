# Wedding day surprises for the bride or groom that are remembered for years

The wedding day is so carefully planned that a surprise seems impossible. And yet the moments people remember most are often the ones that were not in the plan: an unexpected letter, a song, someone who appears when nobody expected them. If you want to prepare a **wedding day surprise for the bride** or groom, here are ideas for every part of the day.

## In the morning

### The letter before you see each other
A handwritten letter that the other person reads while getting ready. Short, specific, with no greetings-card phrases. It is one of the simplest and most moving surprises.

### A gift with a history
A small object connected to your relationship: the ticket from your first concert, a pebble from the beach where you met, a watch with a date engraved on it.

### A recorded message
A short audio or video message from someone important who cannot be there on the day.

## At the ceremony

### A song they did not expect
Agreed with the musicians: the entrance is played to a song with special meaning.

### Vows they did not know existed
If you had agreed traditional vows, add a line of your own at the end.

### A gesture at the altar
A handkerchief embroidered with a date, a flower from your grandmother's garden.

## At the reception

### A cinematic premiere
Switch off the lights and screen a three- or four-minute film in which the two of you are the protagonists of a love story in another era or another world. For the person who has not seen it, it is an enormous surprise; for the guests, a moment they have not seen at any other wedding.

### A performance prepared in secret
With friends or family: a song, a dance, a few words.

### A toast made in their honour
A short speech, addressed to the other person, in front of everyone.

## At the party

### A switched dance
The first dance begins with the agreed song and, halfway through, changes to another that has a special meaning.

### A surprise guest
Someone nobody expected: a friend who lives far away, a relative who seemed unable to come.

## The film as a surprise: how to do it well

A personalised film is created from one photograph of each person and a conversation about your story. There is one clear rule here: **we always work with the explicit consent of both people who appear in it**. That does not rule out the surprise:

- **The other person knows a film exists** and authorises the use of their image.
- **What they do not know is the story:** you decide the script with us.
- **On the wedding day they see it for the first time**, at the same moment as the guests.

If you want it to be a complete surprise, another option is for friends or family to give the film to the couple, so that both see it for the first time at the reception. More information in [your image and AI](/es/blog/imagen-e-ia-consentimiento-y-derechos).

## Tips for getting the surprise right

1. **Coordinate with the wedding planner or the venue**, especially if there is a screening or music.
2. **One surprise per moment.** Piling them up reduces the impact.
3. **Think about the kind of person the other is.** For someone who does not like being the centre of attention, a more private surprise is better.
4. **Appoint someone responsible** to make sure everything is ready, so you do not have to keep an eye on it yourselves.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best surprise for the bride or groom?
The one connected to your story. A letter, a song or a film starring the two of you usually moves people more than any expensive gift.

### How do I keep the film a surprise if it needs their consent?
Their consent is for appearing in the film; the script can stay secret until the premiere.
