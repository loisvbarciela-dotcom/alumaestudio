# A unique Valentine's Day gift: when a card falls short

Valentine's Day has a poor reputation among those who dislike clichés: overpriced roses, restaurants full of couples at tables for two and gifts that seem to come from the same shop window. And yet it is a wonderful excuse to say something you do not say often. A **unique Valentine's Day gift** does not have to be expensive or twee: it has to be yours.

## First, decide what kind of Valentine's Day you want

- **The one that passes quietly:** a small gesture, with no restaurant and no reservations.
- **The one that is celebrated:** a special plan, a getaway, a dinner that means something.
- **The one that becomes a memory:** a gift you will still be watching in ten years' time.

There is no right answer, but some gifts suit each one better than others.

## Unique Valentine's Day gift ideas

### Spending next to nothing
- **A list of reasons**, specific ones, not generic. ‘Because you laugh at my bad jokes’ is worth more than ‘because you are wonderful’.
- **The dinner from your first date**, cooked at home.
- **A playlist with a story:** each song with a note about why it is there.

### With a plan
- **Avoid the 14th:** celebrate on the 13th or the 15th, with no queues and no set menus.
- **A night away from home** somewhere meaningful: a lighthouse, a country house, the village where you met.
- **A new activity for the two of you**, which neither has tried.

### To last
- **An object with a history**, found with patience.
- **Letters to open through the year.**
- **A film in which you are the stars.** From one photograph of each of you, we create a three- or four-minute film that tells your love story in another era or another world: a dance in the twenties, a meeting in Ancient Rome, a journey through space. With script, direction and editing to cinema standards.

## Why a film on Valentine's Day

Because it turns the most predictable date of the year into something unlike anything else. While other couples open the same box of chocolates, you watch for the first time a story that exists only for you. And next year, and the year after, you can watch it again.
