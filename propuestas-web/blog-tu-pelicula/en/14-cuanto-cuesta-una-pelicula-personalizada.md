# How much does a personalised AI film cost, and what does the price depend on?

It is the logical question, and it deserves a clear answer. A **personalised film** doesn't have a single price because no two are alike: a story with two settings doesn't demand the same as one that crosses three eras. ## What you are paying for

A personalised film is not a filter or a template. Behind it is the work of people at every stage:

1. **The conversation and the script.** Understanding your story and turning it into a structure of scenes that works in three or four minutes.
2. **Shot-by-shot direction.** Generating, reviewing, discarding and regenerating each shot until it says what it has to say and your faces are your own.
3. **Designing the universe.** Era, costume, settings, light, atmosphere.
4. **Editing and sound.** Rhythm, music, effects, colour.
5. **Delivery in the formats you need** for the premiere.

AI greatly reduces the cost of the impossible (filming in Ancient Rome or in outer space would be out of reach), but it doesn't remove the work of direction. That is the difference from what automatic apps do, which we compare in [turning photos into video with AI](/es/blog/convertir-fotos-en-video-con-ia).

| **Final length** | Within the 3–4 minutes, more scenes mean more shots to direct |
| **Timeframe** | A very close date forces the production to be reorganised |

## How to ask for a useful quote

The more information you give us, the tighter the quote. Tell us:

- **The occasion:** wedding, anniversary, proposal, gift.
- **The premiere date** and whether it is fixed.
- **The idea, even if vague:** an era, a place, a tone.
- **Where it will be shown:** a big screen at the reception, a television, a phone.

With that, we reply with a proposal and a schedule.

## Is it expensive?

It depends on what you compare it with. Compared with a personalised object, yes. Compared with what it would cost to shoot a period story with actors, costumes and locations, it is a tiny fraction. And compared with other elements of a wedding, it is one of the few things guests will remember precisely years later.

For group gifts (friends who are getting married, siblings celebrating their parents' golden wedding anniversary), the cost divides easily.
