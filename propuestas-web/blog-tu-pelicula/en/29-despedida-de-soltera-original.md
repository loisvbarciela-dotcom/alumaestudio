# A unique hen or stag party: ideas, and the gift that premieres at the wedding

Hen and stag parties have changed. Fewer and fewer people want awkward costumes and dares designed to embarrass, and more and more groups are looking for a plan that the person getting married will really enjoy. A **unique hen party** (or stag party) is one designed for that particular person, not one that follows the usual script.

Here are ideas for different styles, and a suggestion for a group gift that does not end with the party: it premieres at the wedding.

## The golden rule: think of the person getting married

Before choosing, ask yourselves three questions:

1. **What do they really want to do?** Not what ‘you do’ at a hen party.
2. **Who do they want to spend it with?** A large, varied group or a few very close people.
3. **What budget can everyone in the group manage?** A party should not leave anyone out.

## Unique hen and stag party ideas

### A nature plan
A hike with a meal at the end, a day of surfing, rowing a trainera (a traditional Galician rowing boat) or kayaking. In Galicia, a coastal walk or a day on the islands has everything you need.

### A food plan
A cookery workshop, a wine tasting at a winery, a seafood feast at the harbour. It works with groups of very different ages.

### A cultural plan
A break in a city with a common thread: the cinema they love, music, architecture.

### A plan at home
A country house, home cooking, board games, a film night. Perfect for groups who value conversation.

### A plan with a surprise
A secret itinerary with clues that lead from one place to the next and end in a dinner with people they did not expect to see.

## Ideas best avoided

- Dares that humiliate or make people uncomfortable.
- Plans that leave someone out because of budget or mobility.
- Posting things on social media that the person does not want seen.

## The group gift: the couple's film

A hen or stag party is also when the group of friends usually gives a joint gift. One idea that works very well: **give the couple their own film**.

It is a three- or four-minute film in which the bride and groom are the protagonists of a love story in another era or another world, created from one photograph of each. The group announces it at the party and the film premieres on the wedding day, at the reception, in front of all the guests.

## Frequently asked questions

### What can we do for an alternative hen party?
A plan that suits the person: nature, food, culture or a quiet getaway, with no awkward dares.

### What should we give at a hen party?
A meaningful group gift. The couple's film, announced at the party and premiered at the wedding, is one of the most special options.

### Can it be a surprise for the couple?
The story can be. The use of their image requires their explicit consent.
