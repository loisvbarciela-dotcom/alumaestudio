# Unique anniversary gift: a film about your story

An anniversary is a curious date: it celebrates something that already exists. There is no one to win over and nothing to prove, and that is precisely why the gift is so hard to get right. Flowers and dinner are fine, but they look a lot like last year's.

If you are looking for a **unique anniversary gift**, here are ideas ordered from the least to the most ambitious, and one proposal unlike any other: a film starring the two of you.

## What a good anniversary gift asks for

An anniversary looks back and forward at the same time. The gifts that work best do both:

- **They acknowledge what you have lived:** a place, a song, a joke only the two of you understand.
- **They propose something new:** a plan, an experience, a different way of telling the story.

## Unique anniversary gift ideas

### Going back to the beginning
Repeat the first date, with the same menu or in the same place. If the place no longer exists, rebuild it at home.

### The map of your story
An illustration or a map with the places that matter: where you met, where you lived, where you travelled.

### The letters you haven't written to each other
One letter for each year together. Cheap, slow to write and very hard to forget.

### A getaway with a common thread
Not just any trip: one that connects with something of yours. A grandmother's village, a film you watched together, a book.

### A film in which you are the protagonists
The most cinematic idea: from two photographs, one of each of you, we create a film of three or four minutes that tells your love story in another era or another world. Ancient Rome, the twenties, outer space or a universe invented just for you. With the script, direction and editing of a real film.

## Why a film works so well for an anniversary

- **It can't be repeated:** there is no other like it.
- **It creates a moment:** the premiere, at a dinner or on the sofa at home, is part of the gift.
- **It can be watched in twenty years' time** and will still make sense.
- **It speaks about you without repeating the usual photos:** the photos are only the reference for creating the protagonists.

## Can it be a surprise?

Yes, with one important caveat. A personalised film uses the image of both people, and **we always work with the explicit consent of both**. The usual way of keeping the surprise is for the other person to know that a project exists and to authorise it, but not to know the story: you decide the script with us and it isn't seen until the premiere.

Another option is to give the experience: a card `[to confirm: gift voucher format]` on the day of the anniversary, and create the film together over the following weeks.

## Ideas by anniversary year

- **First anniversary (paper anniversary):** a film imagining how it would have been to meet in another era. Light, romantic, with humour.
- **Five or ten years:** a story that runs through what you have lived, told in metaphors: house moves, trips, children turned into adventure scenes.
- **Twenty-five or fifty years:** if the gift is for your parents or grandparents, see our ideas for [silver and golden wedding anniversaries](/en/blog/regalo-aniversario-padres-bodas-de-oro-y-plata).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which photos are needed?
One recent, sharp photograph of each person. We review them with you before we begin.

### Can we include real details from our story?
Yes. A place, a phrase, an object, a song. We weave them into the script as nods.

### How long is the film?
Between three and four minutes.

### What if our anniversary is next week?
Give the experience on the day and premiere the film when it is ready. The premiere is also a celebration.
