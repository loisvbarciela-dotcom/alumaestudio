# Weddings in Galicia: ideas for a celebration with soul (and a film of your own)

Galicia has everything a wedding needs to be memorable: landscape, stone, sea, good food and a culture with a personality of its own. It also has rain, long distances and traditions worth knowing about. If you are planning one of next season's **weddings in Galicia**, these ideas will help your celebration have soul.

We are a film studio based in Galicia, so at the end we have added an idea that is close to our hearts.

## Venues with character

- **Pazos:** Galician manor houses with gardens, stonework and camellias. They are the classic setting for Galician weddings and call for a refined, understated look.
- **Wineries:** in wine regions such as the Rías Baixas, the Ribeiro or the Ribeira Sacra, a ceremony among the vines and a reception with the house wine.
- **By the sea:** lighthouses, beaches, cliffs. Spectacular, but always with a plan B in case the weather does not cooperate.
- **Country houses and mills:** for intimate weddings, with long tables and a family atmosphere.

## The weather: the guest of honour

In Galicia, rain is part of the landscape. Rather than fearing it, it is better to work it into the plan:

- Choose venues with large, attractive covered areas.
- Have umbrellas ready for your guests, ideally with a design of your own.
- Talk to your photographer and videographer: the light on a cloudy day is soft and flattering.

## Food with roots

The reception meal is an essential part of a Galician wedding. Some ideas to give it an identity:

- **Seafood** at the drinks reception, with a shellfish or octopus stall.
- **Cheeses and wines** from Galician designations of origin, with a short explanation on the table.
- **Traditional desserts** reinterpreted.
- **The queimada:** a flaming spirit punch served with its *conxuro*, an incantation recited aloud. It is one of the moments that guests from elsewhere remember most.

## Music and tradition

- **Galician bagpipes (gaita) and tambourine** as you leave the ceremony or to welcome guests.
- **A traditional piece in a contemporary arrangement** for the first dance.
- **Cantareiras**, traditional women singers, during the drinks reception.

Combined with restraint, traditions give a wedding identity without turning it into a folklore show.

## Details for guests

- Galician tinned seafood with a personalised label.
- A small piece of ceramics from a local workshop.
- A bottle of herb or coffee liqueur.
- A native plant to take home.

## Legends that inspire

Galicia is a land of legends, and some are perfect for telling a love story. San Andrés de Teixido, where, as the saying goes, ‘vai de morto quen non foi de vivo’ (those who did not go in life must go in death). The lighthouses of the Costa da Morte. The Santa Compaña, the ghostly procession of the dead, walking the sunken lanes. The islands, the *meigas* (witches), the *castros* (Iron Age hillforts).

In our short films we have worked with many of these stories, such as *Cita en Teixido* or *Santa Compaña*. You can watch them in [our work](/en/work).

## The idea close to our hearts: a film made in Galicia

One way to bring all of this to the big screen is a personalised film starring the bride and groom. From one photograph of each of you, we create a three- or four-minute film that premieres at the reception. It can be set in Ancient Rome or in outer space… or in a legendary Galicia: a lighthouse in the middle of a storm, a pilgrimage, a misty sunken lane.

We make it from Galicia, in Galician, Spanish or English, and we direct it shot by shot to the same standard as our short films. More ideas for settings in [universes for your love film](/en/blog/universos-para-vuestra-pelicula-de-amor).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best time to marry in Galicia?
The summer months and early autumn tend to have better weather, but also more demand. In spring and autumn there is more availability and beautiful light, with a greater risk of rain.

### What is typical at a Galician wedding?
A generous meal with plenty of seafood, the sound of the gaita and, at many celebrations, the queimada.

### Can you make the film in Galician?
Yes. We work in Galician, Spanish and English.

### Do you make films for weddings outside Galicia?
Yes. The film is created from two photos and delivered for its premiere, so it does not depend on where the wedding takes place.
