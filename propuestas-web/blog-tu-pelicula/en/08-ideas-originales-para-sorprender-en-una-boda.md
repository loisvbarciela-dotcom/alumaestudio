# Original ideas to surprise the guests at your wedding

An original wedding isn't the one with the most things, but the one with things that could only happen at that wedding. Guests have been to plenty; what they will remember is what they hadn't seen before and what made them feel part of your story.

These **original wedding ideas** are ordered by moment of the day. You don't need to do them all: with two or three well chosen, the wedding is remembered.

## On arrival

### A welcome with a story
Instead of the generic sign, a short text telling why you chose that place. If you met nearby, say so.

### Canapés with an origin
A dish that recalls a trip, a grandmother, the kitchen of a house. With a card that explains it.

### Unexpected live music
A quartet that plays your song in a version nobody recognises until the chorus. If the wedding is in Galicia, bagpipers (gaiteiros) who walk in unannounced still work. More local ideas in [weddings in Galicia](/es/blog/bodas-en-galicia-ideas).

## At the ceremony

### Your own vows
Written by you, short and concrete. One anecdote is worth ten adjectives.

### Readings from someone who doesn't expect it
Ask a grandfather, a childhood friend or a younger brother to read something, and let them know in good time so they can prepare.

### A symbolic gesture of your own
Planting a tree, mixing sand from two beaches, signing a book you will read on your tenth anniversary.

## At the reception

### Tables with names and stories
Each table named after a place that matters to you, with a line explaining why.

### A cinematic premiere
Dim the lights and screen a film of three or four minutes in which you are the protagonists, living your story in Ancient Rome, in the twenties or in outer space. It is one of the surprises with the most impact because nobody has seen anything like it before. Ideas on when and how to do it in [what video to show at a wedding](/es/blog/que-video-proyectar-en-una-boda).

### Speeches, with a script
Limit their number and length, and ask those speaking to tell one specific story. A good two-minute speech is worth more than an improvised ten-minute one.

### A toast for everyone
All the guests raise their glasses at once with an agreed phrase. Simple and very effective.

## At the party

### A first dance that becomes everyone's
You start alone and, halfway through the song, the music changes and the whole floor joins in.

### A surprise from friends
A choreography, a video, a song with adapted lyrics. If you want it to be a surprise for the couple, leave it to the group of friends. More ideas for the couple in [surprises for the bride or groom](/es/blog/sorpresa-para-la-novia-o-el-novio).

### A moment's pause
Halfway through the party, slip away for a few minutes on your own. It doesn't surprise the guests, but you will thank yourselves.

## At the end

### A gift people will use
Better something to eat, drink or plant than an object left behind on the table.

### A farewell with a message
A sign, a card or a closing screening: “Thank you for being part of this story.”

## How to choose what to do

Ask yourselves three questions before adding anything:

1. **Is it about us?** If it could be at any wedding, it isn't original.
2. **Does it create a shared moment?** What is lived together is remembered more than what is watched in passing.
3. **Does it fit the time of the day?** A wedding packed with surprises stops surprising.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many surprises can you have at a wedding?
Two or three well chosen. Beyond that, each one takes away from the impact of the last.

### Which idea surprises guests the most?
The ones that haven't been seen at other weddings and are about the couple: a film starring the bride and groom, an unexpected performance, your own vows.
