# Personalised photo gifts: from the mug to a film with your faces

A photo turns any object into a personal gift. That is why **personalised photo gifts** are among the most searched for by couples, parents, friends and grandparents. The problem is that, from seeing so many, many have stopped surprising: the mug, the cushion and the calendar are already in every home.

We look at the classic ideas, the ones that still surprise and one that goes beyond the object: using two photos to create a film.

## The classics (which work if chosen well)

- **Printed album:** still one of the best-loved gifts, especially if it tells a story rather than being a pile of photos.
- **Print or framed picture:** one well-chosen photo, well printed and well framed. Less is more.
- **Calendar:** useful and touching, though it lasts a year.
- **Everyday objects:** mugs, cushions, phone cases. Charming, but hard to turn into a keepsake.

**Tip:** the value of these gifts lies in the selection. A single photo with meaning is worth more than twenty chosen at random.

## Ideas that still surprise

### The story book
An album with text: where each photo was taken, what happened that day, what was said. It becomes a small book of memoirs.

### The illustration from a photo
An illustrator turns a photo into a unique piece. It works very well with photos of places or pets.

### The missing photo
Recreate an old photo today: the same place, the same pose, years later. Together, the two photos tell the passing of time.

### The sound of the photo
Accompany a photo with a code that leads to audio: a song, a message, a voice you miss.

## One step further: from photo to film

All the gifts above show photos. There is another possibility: **using the photos as a starting point to create something that has never existed**.

With two photographs, one of each person, we create a film of three or four minutes in which the couple live their story in another era or another world: a meeting in Ancient Rome, a glance in a twenties ballroom, two people finding each other between planets. It isn't a montage of the photos: the photos are the reference for creating the protagonists, and the story, script and direction are decided with you.

## What matters before giving gifts that use other people's photos

If the gift uses someone's image, think about their privacy. In our case, **we always work with the explicit consent of both people who appear in the film**. If you want to keep the surprise, the surprise can lie in the story: they authorise the use of their image and you decide the script with us.
