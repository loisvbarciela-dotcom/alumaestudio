# Anniversary gift for your parents: ideas for a silver or golden wedding anniversary

Twenty-five years of marriage is a silver wedding anniversary. Fifty is a golden one. They are dates the family wants to celebrate, and the gift carries weight: something pretty isn't enough, it has to be worthy of a shared life.

If you are looking for a **golden wedding anniversary gift**, or a silver one, for your parents or grandparents, here are ideas that move people, from the simplest to a film about their story.

## What tends to move people at these anniversaries

After so many years, most couples don't need things. What they value most is:

- **Feeling recognised:** that someone has taken the time to tell their story.
- **Bringing the family together:** children, grandchildren, lifelong friends.
- **Going back to important places and moments.**

## Gift ideas for a silver or golden wedding anniversary

### Recreating the wedding photo
The same place, the same pose, the same (or similar) clothes. The photo from then and the one from now, together in one frame. Simple and very touching.

### The family book
An album with photos and texts from each child, grandchild or friend telling a memory of the couple. It is prepared in secret by everyone.

### Returning to the honeymoon
A getaway to the place where they spent their honeymoon, or to that place they always talk about and never visited again.

### Renewing the vows
A simple ceremony, with the family, in the church or the place where they married, followed by a meal.

### Messages from those who can't be there
A video with greetings from relatives who live far away and from friends of their youth.

### A film starring them
From one photograph of each, we create a film of three or four minutes that tells their love story as an adventure: a meeting in another era, an impossible journey, a cinematic story in which they recognise themselves. With script, direction and editing, and with the details only the family knows woven in as nods.

## Why a film is a good gift for these dates

- **It speaks about them without repeating the usual:** it isn't another run through old photos.
- **It premieres in front of the family**, at the celebration lunch or dinner.
- **It is a gift from everyone:** the children and grandchildren contribute anecdotes for the script.
- **It lasts for ever**, for the grandchildren and for those who come after.
