# Wedding trends 2027: personalised experiences, cinema and artificial intelligence

Every season brings its colour of the moment, its flower of the year and its list of must-haves that nobody will remember in two years. The trends worth paying attention to are different: the ones that change what a couple wants their guests to feel.

This is not a list of colours. It is what we hear in conversations with couples and professionals ahead of the **2027 wedding** season, with one common idea: the personal weighs more than the spectacular.

## 1. Less protocol, more story

Couples want their wedding to say something about them. You can see it in handwritten vows, in menus that recall a trip, in table names, in the music. The question is no longer “what is done at a wedding?” but “what would we do?”

**How to put it into practice:** choose a common thread (a place, an era, a story) and let it appear in three or four details, not forty.

## 2. The guest experience at the centre

More and more weddings are planned from the point of view of the person attending: downtime handled well, shared moments, surprises that involve everyone. The photo booth falls short, and couples look for moments that are lived together. Specific ideas in [wedding entertainment](/es/blog/entretenimiento-para-bodas).

## 3. Smaller, more carefully made weddings

The intimate wedding has stopped being a plan B. Fewer guests allow a bigger budget per person, better venues and more personal details. We develop this in [ideas for an intimate wedding](/es/blog/boda-intima-ideas).

## 4. A cinematic look

Wedding photography and video have been looking to cinema for years: slower shots, careful colour, story rather than compilation. In 2027 that look reaches other parts of the wedding: invitations that look like posters, *save the date* in the form of a trailer, closing credits on the menu. Examples in [save the date videos](/es/blog/save-the-date-en-video).

## 5. Handmade and local

Craftwork, local produce, suppliers from the place where the wedding is held. In Galicia we see it in pazos (traditional manor houses), wineries, tinned seafood, ceramics and traditional music that blends with the contemporary. Local is not just an aesthetic decision: it is a way of telling where the couple come from.

## 6. Artificial intelligence, used with judgement

AI is already at many weddings, even if you can't see it: in planning, in texts, in seating plans. The interesting trend is another one: using it to create something that was impossible before, without the result looking automatic.

One example is the **personalised film**: from two photographs, one of each person, a film of three or four minutes is created in which the couple live their story in Ancient Rome, in the twenties or in outer space. The key, as in any piece of cinema, lies in the direction: AI executes and people decide.

With AI also comes a new demand: **transparency and consent**. Couples ask, quite rightly, what is done with their photos. The answer has to be clear before anything begins.

## 7. Moments that premiere

Related to all of the above: couples want their wedding to have a moment nobody expects. A video on the big screen, a performance, a surprise from friends. More ideas in [how to surprise the guests at your wedding](/es/blog/ideas-originales-para-sorprender-en-una-boda).

## 8. Longer celebrations

A pre-wedding gathering the night before, lunch the next day, whole weekends. If the wedding lasts longer, so does the story: it is a chance to spread the moments out rather than concentrate everything in the reception.

## What we probably won't see so much of

- Decor identical to that of the last wedding the same guest attended.
- Very long photo montages.
- Favours for guests that end up forgotten on the table.

Not because they are wrong, but because couples are looking for things that speak about them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What will be the big wedding trend in 2027?
Personalisation: weddings that tell the couple's story and think about the guests' experience.

### Is artificial intelligence used at weddings?
Yes, mostly in organisation and, increasingly, in creative pieces such as personalised films. What matters is that there is human direction and consent.

### How far ahead do I need to book suppliers for 2027?
It depends on the area and the season. For high-demand dates, it is wise to start many months ahead, especially with venues and photography.
