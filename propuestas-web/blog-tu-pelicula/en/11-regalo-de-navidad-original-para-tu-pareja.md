# Original Christmas gift for your partner: a film that exists only for the two of you

Every December the same thing happens: the intention to find something special and, at the same time, no time. The result is usually a decent gift bought on the 23rd. If this year you want to get the **original Christmas gift for your partner** right, the key is to start earlier and think of something that isn't in any shop window.

Here are ideas for every budget, and one that exists only for you: a film in which you are the protagonists.

## Four questions before choosing

1. **What has marked your year?** A house move, a trip, a change of job, a loss.
2. **What plan is still pending?** The one that always waits for “when we have time”.
3. **Do they prefer objects or experiences?**
4. **Will it be opened in front of the family or in private?** It changes the kind of gift a great deal.

## Original Christmas gift ideas

### An experience with a date
A concert, a getaway, a class in something that has always amused you. What matters is that it has a date: a plan without a date is forgotten.

### An object with a story
Something linked to a specific memory: an old edition of a book, a piece of craftwork from the place where you met, a print of a city that matters to you.

### Next year's calendar
Not one with photos: one with twelve plans, one per month, already decided.

### The letter
A long, handwritten letter explaining why this year has been important. It costs little money and a lot of time, and that is why it shows.

### A film starring the two of you
From two photographs, one of each person, we create a film of three or four minutes in which you live your story in another era or another world: the twenties, Ancient Rome, outer space or a universe invented for you. With the script, direction and editing of a real film. You won't find another like it.

## The surprise and consent

A personalised film uses the image of both people, and **we always work with the explicit consent of both**. If you want to surprise, the most usual approach is for your partner to know there is a project and to authorise the use of their image, but not to know the story until the premiere. The other option is to give the experience and decide the film together.

## Universe ideas for a Christmas film

- **A twenties Christmas:** railway stations, overcoats, snow, a dance.
- **A winter in another era:** a city lit by lanterns, a market, a reunion.
- **Your universe:** your grandparents' village turned into a fairy-tale setting.
