# Personalised film with AI: what it is, how it's made and what to expect

“Personalised film with AI” can mean very different things. It can be an app that moves the eyes in a photo, a filter that turns you into cartoon characters, or a piece of cinema written, directed and edited around you. All three use artificial intelligence. Only the last is a film.

In this article we explain what we at ALUMA mean by a **personalised film with AI**, what role the machine plays and what role people play, and what you can expect from the result, limits included.

## What it is (and what it isn't)

A personalised film is an audiovisual piece of **three or four minutes** in which the two of you are the protagonists. It has a story, scenes, framing, light, rhythm, music and editing. It is not:

- **A photo video with effects.** The photographs are the reference for creating the protagonists, not the material that gets edited.
- **A template.** There is no ready-made video with gaps for your faces.
- **A documentary about your life.** It is a cinematic allegory: recognisable in its protagonists, extraordinary in everything else.

## What the AI does and what we do

Our working rule is simple: **AI doesn't sign the film — it executes it under our direction.**

| People decide | The AI executes |
|---|---|
| The story and the script, agreed with you | Generating each shot according to our instructions |
| The era, the universe and the tone | Impossible settings: Rome, outer space, another century |
| The framing, light and rhythm of each scene | Costumes, atmosphere, extras |
| Which shot works and which is discarded | The variations we ask for until we get it right |
| The edit, the music and the sound | — |

Put another way: the machine composes and renders; the artistic decisions and the responsibility for the result are human.

## How it's made

In three steps (we tell the whole story in [how a love film is made from two photos](/en/blog/como-se-hace-una-pelicula-con-dos-fotos)):

1. **Two photos and a conversation.** One photograph of each person and your story: how you met, what connects you, what you want people to feel.
2. **The film is decided together.** Script, era, tone and key moments, agreed before anything is produced.
3. **A premiere that is yours alone.** Shot-by-shot direction, editing and delivery of a piece made exclusively for your occasion.

## What you can expect from the result

### That you recognise yourselves
Faithfulness to your faces is a central criterion. We review the photos before we begin and discard any shot in which it isn't you. That is why it matters to choose the starting images well.

### The language of cinema
Considered shots, not a string of clips. A film of three or four minutes has a beginning, a middle and an end, however short.

### A universe you couldn't film
The value of AI lies in what would be out of reach with a camera and an ordinary budget: eras, places, scale. That is where it shines most.

### Honest limits
Generative AI still has limits: some gestures, complex physical interactions or very specific details don't always come out first time. We deal with that by directing, discarding and regenerating, and by adapting the script to tell the same thing another way when needed. We would rather explain this up front than promise what cannot be guaranteed.

## How it differs from animating a photo with an app

Photo-animation apps produce a few seconds of movement over a still image. They can be charming, but they don't build a story, they don't keep characters consistent from scene to scene and they have no editing. We compare them in detail in [animating a photo with AI or making a film](/en/blog/animar-fotos-con-ia-o-crear-una-pelicula).

## Your image, with your permission

A personalised film uses your image, so transparency is not optional. **We work with the explicit consent of both people**, agree how the photographs and the film will be used before we begin, and comply with the EU AI Act, with traceability of what is generated. More detail in [your image and AI](/en/blog/imagen-e-ia-consentimiento-y-derechos) and on our [ethics](/en/ethics) page.

## Which occasions it suits

- **Weddings:** screened at the reception, it is a premiere shared with every guest.
- **Anniversaries:** a way of telling your story without repeating the usual photos.
- **Proposals:** the question, inside a film.
- **Special gifts:** for a couple, for parents celebrating their golden wedding anniversary, for friends who are getting married.
