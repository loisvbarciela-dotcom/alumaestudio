# How a love film is made from two photos, step by step

The question we are asked most is always the same: *are two photos really enough?* They are enough to begin with. One photograph of each person is the reference from which we create the protagonists. Everything else — the story, the era, the tone, the moments that matter — comes from a conversation and a great many directing decisions.

This article explains, with no shortcuts and no grand promises, how we turn two photos into a **3–4 minute film starring the two of you**, made to premiere at a wedding, an anniversary, a proposal or as a gift.

## What we need: two photographs and a story

The starting point is deliberately simple:

- **Two photographs**, one of each person.
- **A conversation** about the two of you: how you met, what connects you, what you want anyone watching the film to feel.
- **The occasion and the date** of the premiere, because they set the pace of the whole project.

You don't need a photo shoot, or any filming, or any knowledge of cinema or artificial intelligence. That is our job.

## Step 1. The conversation

Before we think about shots, we think about people. In the first conversation we want to understand which story deserves to be told. Sometimes it is a literal one: the train where you first saw each other, the trip that changed everything. Other times it is a metaphor: two people who meet in another era, on another planet, in another century, and still recognise each other.

We ask things like:

- Which gesture, phrase or place defines you as a couple?
- Is there something your guests don't know that you would like to tell them?
- What tone are you after: epic, tender, funny, mysterious?
- Where will it be shown: on a big screen at the reception, at an intimate dinner, on a phone?

## Step 2. Reviewing the photographs together

A personalised film rests on one idea: **that you recognise yourselves**. That is why the faithfulness of your faces is a central criterion throughout the process, and why we review the photos before we begin.

The best are usually recent, sharp portraits, with the face well lit and no sunglasses or anything else covering the features. If one doesn't work, we tell you and choose another together. We explain it in detail in [how to choose the two photos for your film](/en/blog/como-elegir-las-fotos-para-vuestra-pelicula).

## Step 3. Choosing the universe

This is where the fun begins. Love changes eras, and you are the protagonists. Some starting points we suggest:

- **Ancient Rome:** an encounter among marble and great empires.
- **The twenties:** trains, ballrooms and cities awakening.
- **Outer space:** two people finding each other between worlds.
- **Your universe:** a memory, a fantasy or an impossible combination.

It is not a closed catalogue. If you picture yourselves in a lighthouse on Galicia's Costa da Morte or in a fifties musical, we can talk about it. In [universes for your love film](/en/blog/universos-para-vuestra-pelicula-de-amor) we explain what works best in each.

## Step 4. The script, agreed with you

Once the story and the universe are defined, we write the script: the structure, the scenes and the key moments. **The film is decided together.** We present it to you, you comment on it and we adjust it before producing anything.

This step is what separates a film from a template. We don't fill in the blanks of a ready-made video: we build a story that only makes sense with the two of you in it.

## Step 5. Directing shot by shot

Artificial intelligence is an extraordinary tool, but it doesn't decide the film: AI doesn't sign the film — it executes it. We direct every shot by the same criteria used to direct a camera: framing, light, rhythm, intention.

In practice, that means generating, discarding, correcting and generating again until each scene says what it has to say and your faces are your own. It is painstaking, hand-crafted work, shot by shot, and it is why the result looks nothing like a phone filter.

## Step 6. Editing, sound and premiere

With the scenes ready, we edit the film: pacing, transitions, music and sound, until we reach a piece of **three or four minutes**, high quality and made exclusively for your occasion.

Then comes the best part: the premiere. At a wedding it is usually screened during the reception; at an anniversary, over dinner; at a proposal, at exactly the right moment. ## What we don't do

- **We never use your image without permission.** We work with the explicit consent of both people and agree how the photos and the film will be used before we begin, in line with the EU AI Act. More detail in [your image and AI](/en/blog/imagen-e-ia-consentimiento-y-derechos).
- **We don't make a slideshow with effects.** The photos are the reference, not the content.
- **We don't reuse your film** for other purposes without your authorisation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do photos taken on a phone work?
Yes, as long as they are sharp and the face is clearly visible. The quality of the light matters more than the camera.

### Can we decide what happens in the film?
Yes. Script, era, tone and events are agreed with you from the start, guided by our cinematic judgement.

### Can it be a surprise?
The story can be a surprise; the use of your image cannot. Both people who appear must give their explicit consent, even if only one of them knows the script.

### Is it only for weddings?
No. A wedding is a perfect premiere, but so is an anniversary, a proposal or a special gift.
