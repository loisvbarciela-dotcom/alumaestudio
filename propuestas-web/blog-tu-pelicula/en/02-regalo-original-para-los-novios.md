# Unique wedding gift for the couple: a film in which they are the stars

Some couples are easy to buy for. Others already have everything: the home set up, the honeymoon booked and a gift list that was filled in two days. For them, an envelope is practical, but it is not remembered. If you are looking for a **unique wedding gift for the couple**, the secret is not price or size, but something that could only ever be theirs.

We look at what makes a wedding gift memorable, some ideas that work, and why a film starring the bride and groom is one of the few gifts that can never be repeated.

## What makes a wedding gift memorable

After many weddings, the gifts people remember have three things in common:

1. **They are personal.** They speak about the couple, not about weddings in general.
2. **They create a moment.** They are opened, watched or lived in front of other people.
3. **They last.** They still make sense at the first anniversary and at the tenth.

A set of glasses ticks the third box. A joke at the reception ticks the second. The hard part is ticking all three at once.

## Original wedding gift ideas for a couple who have everything

- **A shared experience:** dinner somewhere special to them, a night in a lighthouse, a tasting. It works if you know their tastes well.
- **An object with a story:** a piece of craftwork, a first edition of the book that brought them together, an illustration of the house where they will live.
- **A group gift:** several friends or siblings pool what they would each give separately and offer something none of them could manage alone.
- **An audiovisual piece:** a video of good wishes, a montage of photos from a lifetime or, one step further, a film in which they are the protagonists.

## Why a film is a different kind of gift

A photo montage looks back at what has already happened. A **personalised film** imagines what could happen: the couple meeting in Ancient Rome, in a twenties ballroom or on a space station, with their own faces, in a story of three or four minutes shot in the language of cinema.

We start with two photographs, one of each person, and a conversation about their story. Then we write the script, direct the AI shot by shot and edit an exclusive piece. We explain it in detail in [how a love film is made from two photos](/en/blog/como-se-hace-una-pelicula-con-dos-fotos).

It meets all three conditions: it can't be repeated, it creates a moment (the premiere) and it gains value with the years.

## How to give it without spoiling anything: the question of consent

We need to be clear here. A personalised film uses the couple's image, and **we always work with the explicit consent of both people**. It is not a formality: it is what the EU AI Act requires, and it is the right thing to do.

- **Surprise them with the story, not with the fact that it exists:** the couple agree to appear, but you decide the script with us and they don't see it until the premiere.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the couple take part in the story?
Yes, and it usually works better. If you would rather keep the script secret, we build it with you and they only give their consent for the use of their image.

### Can several friends give it together?
Yes. It is one of the most common formats: a group gift that none of you could give on your own.

### What if the wedding is only a few weeks away?
Write to us with the date. If there isn't time to premiere it at the wedding, it is given as an experience and premiered afterwards, for example at the first anniversary.

### Do the couple need professional photos?
No. One recent, sharp portrait of each of them is enough.
