# From Ancient Rome to outer space: universes for your love film

Love changes eras. The protagonists are you. That is the idea behind a personalised film: telling your story in a world you could never film. Choosing that world is one of the most enjoyable decisions in the process, and one of the most important, because it sets the tone for everything else.

These are the universes we suggest as a starting point. They are not a closed catalogue: they are ideas to start your imagination.

## Ancient Rome

**An encounter among marble and great empires.** Your story becomes a legend that travels through the centuries.

- **Tone:** epic, solemn, on a grand scale.
- **Possible scenes:** a market full of life, a temple at dusk, a farewell before a battle, a reunion in a courtyard of columns.
- **Suits:** couples who have overcome obstacles, long-distance stories, weddings with a sense of grand celebration.

## The twenties

**A glance among trains, ballrooms and cities awakening.** Elegance, adventure and the beginning of something unrepeatable.

- **Tone:** elegant, romantic, with a touch of adventure.
- **Possible scenes:** a steaming station platform, a dance in a ballroom with an orchestra, an open-top car, a rooftop above a lit-up city.
- **Suits:** couples who love classic cinema, music and travel. A very good universe for a wedding or a proposal.

## Outer space

**Two people finding each other between worlds.** A journey where neither distance nor time can keep them apart.

- **Tone:** contemplative or adventurous, depending on what you want.
- **Possible scenes:** an orbital station, an unknown planet, a signal crossing the galaxy, a window with a view of the Earth.
- **Suits:** couples who have lived at a distance, people with a science-fiction soul, stories about finding each other against all odds.

## Your universe

**The future, a memory, a fantasy or an impossible combination.** The world is written around what brings you together.

Some ideas we have found beautiful:

- **Legendary Galicia:** a lighthouse on the Costa da Morte, a misty *corredoira* (sunken country lane), the pilgrimage of San Andrés de Teixido turned into the setting for a promise.
- **The cinema you love:** a western, a musical, a black-and-white detective film.
- **Your work turned into an adventure:** a doctor and an engineer on an expedition; two teachers in an infinite library.
- **An impossible combination:** beginning in imperial Rome and ending on Mars.

## How to choose your universe

Ask yourselves these three questions:

1. **What tone do we want?** Epic, tender, funny, mysterious. The universe should serve that tone.
2. **Where will it be shown?** An epic film shines on the big screen at a reception; a more intimate one suits a dinner for two.
3. **Which metaphor tells our story best?** If you met far from home, a journey. If it took you a long time to find each other, a quest. If you are opposites, two worlds colliding.

If you are not sure, that is perfectly fine: in our first conversation we will find out together.

## What matters more than the setting

The universe is the wrapping. What makes a film moving is the story, and recognising yourselves in it. That is why the faithfulness of your faces is a central criterion and why we agree the script with you before production. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can we mix several eras in one film?
Yes. Two or three well-chosen settings fit into three or four minutes. More settings mean more production, and that is reflected in the budget.

### Can we suggest a universe that is not on the list?
Yes. The list is a set of starting points, not a closed catalogue.

### Are the costumes and settings realistic?
We design them with a cinematic eye for each era. It is an allegory, not a historical documentary, but we take care over the details.
