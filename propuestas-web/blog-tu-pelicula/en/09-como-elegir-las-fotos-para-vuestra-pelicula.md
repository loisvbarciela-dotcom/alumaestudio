# How to choose the two photos for your personalised film (checklist)

A personalised film begins with two photographs, one of each person. They are the reference from which we create the protagonists, so a good part of whether you recognise yourselves on screen depends on them. The good news: you don't need a photographer. The less good news: not every photo is equally useful.

This guide explains **which photos to use for an AI video** like ours, with a checklist you can follow phone in hand.

## Why the photo matters so much

Artificial intelligence doesn't see the person: it sees an image. Everything the image doesn't show well — a feature in shadow, part of the face covered, an odd angle — the AI has to invent, and what is invented looks less like you. That is why the faithfulness of your faces, a central criterion of our work, starts here.

## The checklist

### 1. One person per photo
One photograph of each of you, separately. If you only have photos together, look for one in which each face is large and sharp; we can work from it, but one per person is better.

### 2. Recent
It should look like you do today (or how you want to appear). If someone has had a haircut, grown a beard or wears glasses every day, the photo should show it.

### 3. Front-facing, or nearly
A front-facing or slightly turned portrait. Full profiles and photos taken from below make a likeness harder.

### 4. The face as the subject
The face should take up a good part of the image. A full-length photo on the beach, with a tiny face, doesn't work as the main reference.

### 5. Good, soft light
Natural daylight, without harsh shadows on the face. A window to one side is perfect. Avoid backlighting and direct flash.

### 6. Sharp
No blur or motion. Zoom in on the photo on your phone: if the eyes look sharp, we are on the right track.

### 7. Nothing covering the features
No sunglasses, caps, masks, hands on the face or hair over the eyes.

### 8. A natural expression
A relaxed expression or a slight smile. Grimaces, joke faces or very wide laughs give less information about your features.

### 9. No filters
No beauty filters, skin smoothing or effects. The AI would take the filter for your face.

### 10. The original file
Send the original photo, not a screenshot or a compressed version from a messaging app, if you can avoid it.

## Common mistakes

- **The favourite photo, but an old one.** If it is ten years old, it isn't you today.
- **The wide-angle selfie, taken very close.** It distorts the nose and jaw.
- **The photo from a friends' wedding under disco lighting.** Odd colours, harsh shadows.
- **Cropping a tiny group photo.** When you enlarge it, the detail is lost.

## If you don't have a suitable photo

Take one in two minutes: next to a window, in daylight, the phone at eye level, face front, no sunglasses. Two or three shots and we choose the best. Before we begin we review the photos together and, if any doesn't work, we tell you.

## What about clothes, hair and make-up?

The clothes and the setting are created by the film (Roman togas, twenties suits, space suits), so what you wear in the photo doesn't matter. Hairstyle does count if it is a defining feature. As for make-up, the more natural the better.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do photos taken on a phone work?
Yes. A current phone with good light is more than enough.

### Can we send more than one photo per person?
Yes, it can help in choosing the best one. The film starts from one main reference per person.

### Can old photos be used for an anniversary film?
To recreate the couple when they were young, we can talk about it; the result depends on the quality of the photo. Mention it when you tell us your idea.

### What happens if the photo isn't good?
We tell you before we begin and choose another together. We don't start production with a reference that doesn't work.
