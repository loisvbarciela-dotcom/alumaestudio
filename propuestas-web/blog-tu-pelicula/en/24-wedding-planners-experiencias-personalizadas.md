# Wedding planners: how to set your offer apart with personalised experiences

Couples come to a wedding planner with a request that is heard more and more often: ‘we want something nobody has seen’. They say it knowing they have seen many weddings on social media, and that most look alike. For a planner, that is both a challenge and an opportunity: the difference no longer lies in the decoration, but in the ability to create moments that speak of the couple.

This article gathers ideas to **set your offer apart as a wedding planner** and explains how a personalised film can become one of those moments.

## Why personalisation is the new advantage

- **Couples compare on social media** and quickly spot what is repeated.
- **Guests have been to many weddings**, and value what they have not seen.
- **Word-of-mouth recommendation** is born from a specific moment that people talk about the next day.

An offer that includes unique moments is remembered and recommended. An offer that only coordinates suppliers competes on price.

## Five ways to personalise a wedding (beyond the decoration)

1. **The narrative:** helping the couple find a common thread (a place, an era, a story) that appears at three or four moments of the day.
2. **The guest experience:** designing the day with the people attending in mind: dead time well handled, shared moments.
3. **Rituals of their own:** vows written by the couple, symbolic gestures with meaning.
4. **Food with a story:** dishes that say something about the couple or the place.
5. **The premiere:** an audiovisual moment nobody expects.

## The personalised film as a service in your offer

A personalised film is a three- or four-minute piece in which the couple star in a cinematic story: a meeting in Ancient Rome, a dance in the twenties, a journey between planets. It is created before the wedding from two photographs and a conversation, and premieres during the reception.

### Why it fits a planner's offer

- **It is exclusive:** no other couple will have the same one.
- **It is easy to integrate:** it needs no crew on the wedding day, only a screen and a slot in the running order.
- **It does not compete with other suppliers:** it does not replace the photographer or the videographer; it complements them. We explain this in [wedding video or personalised film](/es/blog/video-de-boda-o-pelicula-personalizada).
- **It creates a shared moment** that every guest remembers.
- **It sets you apart** from offers that only provide the usual.

- **At the first meeting**, as part of the ‘experiences’ in your offer.
- **With the example film** on a tablet or laptop: it is worth more than any explanation.
- **As a gift from parents or friends**, if the couple have already closed their budget.
