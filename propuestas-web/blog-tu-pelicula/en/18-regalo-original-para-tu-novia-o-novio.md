# A unique gift for your girlfriend (or boyfriend) that you can't buy in a shop

The search for a **unique gift for your girlfriend** or boyfriend usually starts with a list of products and ends with the feeling that nothing is personal enough. That is normal: what is unique is not in the catalogue. It is in what you know about the other person and nobody else does.

These ideas start from that premise. Some cost very little; others take more preparation. None can be bought with a click.

## The golden rule: give something that shows you listen

The best gift answers something the other person said months ago and thought you had forgotten. For a few weeks, note down on your phone everything they mention: a place they would like to visit, a book, a childhood memory, a film. The gift is in there.

## Unique gift ideas for your partner

### For someone who values memories
- **The lost object:** that toy, record or book from their childhood that no longer exists at home. Finding it takes time, and it shows.
- **The photo you never took:** recreate an important moment in the place where it happened.

### For someone who values experiences
- **A day designed just for them:** breakfast at their favourite place, an activity they have always wanted to try and a dinner that means something.
- **A getaway with a thread running through it:** the setting of a film you both love, their grandparents' village, a lighthouse.

### For someone who values the handmade
- **‘Open when…’ letters:** ‘when you're sad’, ‘when we've had an argument’, ‘when we're celebrating something’.
- **A small book of your story**, even if you print it at home.

### For someone who already has everything
- **A film in which the two of you are the stars.** From one photograph of each of you, we create a three- or four-minute film that tells your love story in another era or another world. Script, direction and editing to cinema standards, with your faces. There is no other like it.

## Why a film is a different kind of gift

- **It has no rival:** it cannot be compared with another gift, because there is no other like it.
- **It creates a moment:** watching it together for the first time is part of the gift.
- **It speaks of you** without repeating the same old photos.
- **It can be watched again** every anniversary.

## How to give it without losing the surprise

A personalised film uses the image of both of you, and **we always work with the explicit consent of both people**. To keep the surprise, the usual approach is for your partner to know there is a project and to authorise the use of their image, but not to know the story.
