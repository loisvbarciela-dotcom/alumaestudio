# Intimate wedding ideas: how to make a small guest list unforgettable

An **intimate wedding** is not a small wedding out of necessity, but a decision: fewer guests, so that each one counts for more. Without the logistics of a wedding for two hundred, everything can be looked after in greater detail, and the moments are lived differently. These ideas help you make the most of that scale.

## Why more and more couples choose an intimate wedding

- **More budget per guest:** better food, better wine, better details.
- **More time with each person:** you can talk to everyone.
- **More freedom to choose the venue:** places that could not host a large wedding.
- **Less stress** in the planning.

## Venues that shine with a small guest list

- **A house with a history:** a pazo, a stone house, a family estate.
- **A restaurant closed just for you**, with the chef cooking a menu designed for the occasion.
- **A place in nature:** a viewpoint, a lighthouse, a beach out of season.
- **A winery:** the ceremony among the vines and the meal at one long table.

Galicia has perfect venues for this format; ideas in [weddings in Galicia](/en/blog/bodas-en-galicia-ideas).

## A more personal ceremony

With few guests, the ceremony can become a conversation:

- **Let each guest say a sentence** or read a wish for the couple.
- **Your own vows**, without hurry.
- **A celebrant close to you:** a friend or a relative.
- **A ritual with meaning**, such as an object passed from hand to hand.

## A meal without protocol

- **A single long table**, where everyone can see one another.
- **A menu with a story**, explained by the person who cooked it.
- **Time to linger at the table after the meal**, which at an intimate wedding is the best part.

## Details that would be impossible at a large wedding

- **A personal letter for each guest.**
- **A gift chosen for each person**, not the same favour for everyone.
- **A portrait of each guest** by a photographer during the afternoon.

## A premiere for a few, with all the cinema

An intimate wedding does not mean giving up the big moments. One idea that works especially well in this format: **screen a film starring the couple** while you linger at the table after the meal.

It is a three- or four-minute piece in which you live your story in another era or another world, created from one photograph of each of you, with a script agreed with you and the AI directed shot by shot. In a small room, with the people closest to you, the premiere is experienced almost like a neighbourhood cinema: everyone together, in silence, and then the conversation.

What is more, at an intimate wedding there are people who could not attend. The film can be shared afterwards with the family and friends who were not there, if you decide so.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many guests does an intimate wedding have?
There is no official number. People usually speak of an intimate wedding or micro wedding when only the closest family and a few friends are invited.

### Is an intimate wedding cheaper?
Not necessarily. Many couples keep the budget and invest it in more quality per guest.

### Does a film make sense at a small wedding?
Yes. In an intimate setting, the premiere is felt with more emotion, and the film can be shared afterwards with those who could not attend.
